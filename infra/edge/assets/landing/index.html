Plan, track, discover

Short, regular practice is what helps a puppy learn. Toe Beans makes it easy to keep it going, whoever's at home.

Plan

What should we do today?

Set a few short sessions for the week. Today shows what's planned, what's overdue and what's done, for each dog.

Track

How is our puppy progressing?

Log a practice in seconds. History puts training, events like a barking fit or a visitor, and care in one timeline.

Discover

What else could we try?

A library of reward-based exercises, from the name game to calm car rides. Each comes in small steps, with what to look for and when to ask a vet or trainer, ready to plan.

Practice isn't a test

Logging a session never means claiming it went well. Say how it went only if you want to, and "not yet" is just part of learning, never a failure.

Made for the whole household

  • Everyone on the same page

    Invite your household by email or a link. Everyone sees the same plan, and every dog keeps its own history.

  • Reminders that arrive

    Practice, vaccinations, deworming and vet visits remind you on your phone, even with the app closed.

  • Kind, reward-based training

    Gradual steps and plenty of treats. No punishment, ever.

  • Private and free

    No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Your household's records are yours, and you can download them whenever you like.
