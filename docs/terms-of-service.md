# Terms of service

These terms are the agreement between you and Veli Gebrev ("we", "us"), who runs Toe Beans. You
accept them when you start using Toe Beans, by ticking the box that asks you to, and we keep a
record of which version you accepted and when. Please read them with the
[privacy policy](/privacy), which says what we do with your information.

## 1. What Toe Beans is

Toe Beans is a free app for training and caring for a dog, shared by the people in one household.
You can keep dog profiles, plan and log training, track care, keep a cue dictionary, invite
caregivers and get reminders on your phone.

It's run by one person, without charge and without any promise of how long it will run (§8).

## 2. Your account

- You must be **18 or older** to use Toe Beans. You confirm it when you accept these terms.
- Sign in with your own Google account or email address, and keep your sign-in to yourself.
  You're responsible for what's done with your account.
- Tell us at support@toebeans.co.za if you think someone else has used it.

## 3. Households

- Everything about your dogs belongs to a **household**. Each member can see and change all its
  dogs and records, and invite others.
- **Invite only people you trust** with your household's information and photos. Owners can remove
  caregivers; any member can leave.
- If you leave, what you recorded stays with the household. If you delete your account, it shows as
  from "a former member" (privacy policy §6).
- You can be in one household at a time.

## 4. What you add

- What you add (records, notes, photos) stays yours. You let us store it, copy it to backups and
  show it to your household's members, only so Toe Beans can work.
- Add only photos and content you have the right to share, and nothing about other people that
  they wouldn't want shared.
- If you think something in Toe Beans infringes your rights, such as a photo of yours used without
  permission, write to support@toebeans.co.za saying what it is and why. We'll look into it and
  remove it if it does.
- **Don't use Toe Beans** to break the law, to harass anyone, to store content unrelated to caring
  for your dogs, to try to reach another household's data, or to overload, probe or attack the
  service. We may remove content, or suspend or close an account, that does any of this. Unless the
  harm is serious, we'll tell you first and give you the chance to put it right.

## 5. Training and care content is guidance

- The exercise library favours reward-based, gradual training. It's general guidance, not
  professional advice for your dog.
- **Toe Beans isn't veterinary advice.** Care milestones and reminders are what your household sets
  up, not medical prescriptions. Ask your vet about your dog's health, vaccinations and medication,
  and a qualified behaviourist about fear or aggression.
- **Don't rely on reminders alone** for anything your dog's health depends on. They depend on your
  phone, its settings, your connection and services outside our control, and may be late or not
  arrive.

## 6. The service as it is

Toe Beans is free and provided as it is. We do our best to keep it working, secure and backed up,
but we can't promise it will always be available, free of faults, or that nothing will ever be
lost. To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for any loss that comes from using or not
being able to use Toe Beans, and nothing in these terms limits rights you have under South African
law that can't be limited.

## 7. Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time from *More → Delete account* (privacy policy §6). We may
close an account as §4 says.

## 8. If Toe Beans closes

If we decide to stop running Toe Beans, we'll tell you by email or in the app at least 30 days
before, and send you a copy of your household's data if you ask during that time.

## 9. Changes to these terms

When these terms change we'll update the date at the top, and the app asks you to accept the new
version the next time you open it. If a change matters to how you use Toe Beans, we'll tell you in
the app or by email at least 30 days before it takes effect. If you don't agree, you can delete
your account instead of accepting (*More → Delete account*).
