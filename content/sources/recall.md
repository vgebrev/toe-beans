# Recall

- [How to train your dog to come back when called](https://www.dogstrust.org.uk/dog-advice/training/basics/recall-training) — Dogs Trust
- [Dog recall training](https://www.bluecross.org.uk/advice/dog/behaviour-and-training/dog-recall-training) — Blue Cross
- [How can I teach my dog recall (to come when called)?](https://kb.rspca.org.au/categories/companion-animals/dogs/training/how-can-i-teach-my-dog-recall-to-come-when-called) — RSPCA Australia

Common ground:

- Reward-based training works better for recall than aversive methods; Blue Cross advises against aversive equipment entirely.
- Reward every return generously, and start at short range indoors.
- Avoid calling the puppy away from things they love, or to something unpleasant, unless it's unsafe.
- A long line gives freedom safely while recall is learned outdoors.
- No dog has 100% recall (RSPCA Australia); adolescence often brings a dip.
