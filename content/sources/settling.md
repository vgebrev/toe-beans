# Settling

- [My dog won't settle: teaching your dog to be calm and relaxed](https://www.battersea.org.uk/pet-advice/dog-advice/my-dog-wont-settle-teaching-your-dog-be-calm-and-relaxed) — Battersea
- [Teaching Calm: SOFT and Handling Exercises](https://vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/dog-behavior-and-training---teaching-calm---soft-and-handling-exercises) — VCA Animal Hospitals
- Cross-check: Dr Karen Overall's Relaxation Protocol (as described by trainers)

Common ground:

- A portable mat used only for this; drop treats on the mat so it becomes a good place.
- Reward small steps: interest in the mat, paws on it, lying down, then signs of relaxing (hip rolled, chin down).
- Build duration slowly with calm, low-key rewards; keep your own energy low.
- Over-tired puppies struggle to settle: they need a lot of sleep.
- No scolding; if they wander off, wait and reward the return.
