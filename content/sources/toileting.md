# Toilet training

- [How to toilet train your puppy or dog](https://www.rspca.org.uk/adviceandwelfare/pets/dogs/training/toilettraining) — RSPCA (UK)
- [How can I toilet train my puppy or adult dog?](https://kb.rspca.org.au/categories/companion-animals/dogs/caring-for-my-dog/how-can-i-toilet-train-my-puppy-or-adult-dog) — RSPCA Australia
- [How do I toilet train my dog?](https://www.royalkennelclub.com/your-dog/dog-training/get-started/dog-training-and-games/how-do-i-toilet-train-my-dog/) — The Kennel Club
- Cross-check: Dogs Trust's guide (via Petplan)

Common ground:

- Take the puppy out often: after waking, eating, playing and on a regular schedule.
- Reward within a few seconds of finishing outside, not when back indoors.
- An optional cue word said while they go can later help on cue.
- Clean accidents with an enzyme (non-ammonia) cleaner; never tell the puppy off for them.
- Never withhold food or water to control toileting (Dogs Trust).
- Bladder control isn't complete until about 12 months (RSPCA UK).
