# Loose-lead walking

- [Teaching your dog to walk on a loose leash](https://www.maddiesfund.org/teaching-your-dog-to-walk-on-a-loose-leash.htm) — Maddie's Fund
- [How to stop your dog pulling the lead](https://www.rspcaqld.org.au/resources/pet-care/how-to-stop-your-dog-pulling-the-lead-MCDU4OMEJLXZBIZEIZ7P3N2PSFOQ) — RSPCA Queensland
- Cross-check: Ontario SPCA, Purina and Rover lead-training guides

Common ground:

- Get the puppy comfortable with the collar or harness and lead first, with treats.
- Reward often for being next to you while the lead is slack, every few steps at first.
- When the lead goes tight, stop and wait (or turn and go the other way); carry on once it's slack.
- Start indoors or in the garden, then quiet streets, then busier places.
- Equipment: a flat collar or a front-attaching harness; no extending leads for training.
- Reward the slack lead rather than punishing the tight one.
