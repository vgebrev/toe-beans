- [How should I introduce my new dog or puppy to my existing cat?](https://kb.rspca.org.au/categories/companion-animals/cats/caring-for-my-cat/how-should-i-introduce-my-new-dog-or-puppy-to-my-existing-cat) — RSPCA Australia
- [How to introduce a new puppy to your cat](https://www.cats.org.uk/cats-blog/how-to-introduce-a-new-puppy-to-your-cat) — Cats Protection

Common ground:

- Gradual, owner-led introductions give the best chance of a friendly relationship (RSPCA Australia).
- Scent swapping before any face-to-face meeting (International Cat Care); a barrier (gate, glass, crate) for first sightings.
- The cat needs high places and puppy-free rooms to retreat to.
- Avoid even one bad meeting: a chase or a fright can set things back a long way.
- Never leave them together unsupervised, however well it's going.
