Common ground:

- Barking is normal communication; find out why first (boredom, loneliness, fear, alerting, attention, frustration).
- Punishment tends to make barking worse (RSPCA NSW); use rewards for quiet and meet the underlying need.
- Attention-seeking barking: don't pay it off; reward quiet moments instead.
- Trigger barking: gradual exposure at a level the puppy copes with, rewarding calm.
- See a vet first if barking changes suddenly (pain, illness); avoid trainers who use punishment.
