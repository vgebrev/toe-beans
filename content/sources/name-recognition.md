# Name recognition

- [How to Teach Your Dog Their Name](https://www.akc.org/expert-advice/training/how-to-teach-dog-name/) — American Kennel Club
- [How To Teach Your Dog Their Name: Step-by-Step Guide](https://www.purina.co.uk/pet-care-articles-and-advice/articles/expert-dog-care/dog-behaviour/dog-training/teach-your-puppy) — Purina UK
- Cross-check: Best Friends, Kinship, K9 of Mine, Andrea Arden's name-recognition handout

Common ground:

- Say the name once, when the puppy isn't already looking; mark and reward the moment they look.
- Small, tasty treats, kept out of sight, so the puppy answers the sound and not the food.
- Start somewhere quiet, then add distance, other rooms and outdoors one at a time.
- Keep the name for good things: don't use it to tell off, or to call the puppy away from fun.
- "Learn it in a day" timelines in blogs have no evidence behind them; puppies vary.
