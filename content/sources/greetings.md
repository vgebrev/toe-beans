# Calm greetings

- [How to stop your dog jumping up](https://www.battersea.org.uk/pet-advice/dog-advice/how-teach-your-dog-not-jump) — Battersea
- [How to stop your dog from jumping up on people](https://www.akc.org/expert-advice/training/how-to-stop-your-dog-from-jumping-up-on-people/) — American Kennel Club
- [Why does my dog jump up?](https://vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/dog-behavior-problems-greeting-behavior-jumping-up) — VCA Animal Hospitals

Common ground:

- Jumping up is a normal way to say hello that attention keeps rewarding.
- Attention comes only with four paws on the floor; turn away quietly if they jump, and greet again once paws are down.
- Teach a sit (or just "four on the floor") as the greeting instead, and reward it before the jump happens.
- Use a gate or lead with visitors while learning; everyone has to respond the same way.
- Don't push, shout or frighten the puppy.
